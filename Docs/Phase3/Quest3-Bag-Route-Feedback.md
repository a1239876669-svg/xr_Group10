# Quest 3 check for shopping bag contact on the kitchen route

Use Unity 6000.6.4f1, Windows DX11 and Quest Link. Open `Assets/Scenes/Apartment_Detailed_VR.unity`. For an Editor test, select **Apartment Phase 3 → Review Test Storage → Use New Isolated Development Folder** before entering Play. Test in the formal apartment with its own bag and rig. This sheet starts with every headset check pending.

| Step | Action | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | With both hands empty and review tools closed, aim the right ray at a kitchen cabinet door and click the right index trigger. Open and close it, then return to the entrance. | The door swings normally. Hover highlighting clears when the ray moves away. No bag vibration. |
| 2 | In English, use **B → My Items** to choose a bag. Press Grip away from it, then take it within reach with the left Grip. | The missed attempt is silent. The first successful pickup shows the contact explanation for about five visible seconds. Movement and Grip release stay available. |
| 3 | Release, pick up again, release and pick up with the other hand. Open **B → Controls → Review guide → Bag contact**. | The automatic tip appears once. Help contains the same explanation. Only one hand holds the bag. Holding Grip across a menu or focus change still requires release and a new press before a new pickup. |
| 4 | Walk naturally to the kitchen. With your body clear of the wall, let the bag graze a registered wall section and continue forward. Hold contact, then move clear. Repeat with the other hand. | Only the surface near the bag turns red. Slide the bag along the wall and check that the red area follows it and leaves the far end uncoloured. Only the holding hand gives a short pulse. Walking continues. The warning clears when the bag leaves and there is no continuous vibration. |
| 5 | With the bag held, touch a closed west or north cabinet door. Open it using the existing interaction. Put the bag in the now-empty space clear of neighbouring surfaces, then touch the open leaf. Also try an exposed cabinet side and both jambs of the kitchen opening. | Red follows the point of contact on the moving door leaf or side panel. The old door position clears. Unrelated panels retain their normal appearance. Removing the bag restores the correct normal or hovered appearance. |
| 6 | Release the bag during contact and try two neighbouring surfaces with a larger bag. Finish the existing kitchen floor drop and proceed to the measurement step. | Release clears the bag warning and vibration. Leaving just one of two contacted surfaces clears that surface. The existing drop task advances. |
| 7 | Stop and enter Play again. Set **B → Settings → Language → 简体中文** before the first pickup. Open **B → 按键说明 → 任务帮助 → 购物袋接触提示**. | A fresh session shows the automatic tip once in Chinese. Help can be opened again. Verify text clarity in the headset and check that menu or teleport aiming can take priority. |

For a novice session, observe the first trip without asking the participant to touch anything. Afterwards ask “What did you notice while carrying the bag?” and “What did you think had happened?” Use the deliberate cabinet contact in step 5 as a separate functional check. Record hand, bag size, surface, observed behaviour and outcome for each check. Physical vibration, headset legibility and walking comfort require observations on the device.
